## How to verify

1. **Pin the release hash** above. Never pin the tarball's name or a path (`RUL-10` (c)).
2. **Check the tarball's bytes against its `MANIFEST`,** with no Tviburi tool. `spike/2100/consumer-verify.sh TARBALL PIN [RENODE_ROOT]` is the reference, in POSIX `sh` with `sha256sum`:
   - the `MANIFEST`'s sha256 is the pinned hash;
   - `TviburiRelease.cs.template` matches its `file` line;
   - `Tviburi.cs`'s first n bytes match the `overlay` line, and its tail is the template with `@@MANIFEST_SHA256@@` replaced by the `MANIFEST`'s sha256;
   - `LEDGER.jsonl`'s first line names the release, and its other lines hash to `ledger-rows`;
   - the tarball holds exactly those four files, each once (no fifth or hidden member, no duplicate);
   - given the consumer's Renode root, its `Renode.dll` and `Infrastructure.dll` have the sha256s the `MANIFEST`'s `build` lines name. The strings alone cannot name the rebuilt `Infrastructure.dll` (`TVI-WO-2100.measure.md` §3).

   This is the binding check (`RUL-10` (f)). `tools/tviburi-release verify <dir> <pin>` does the same from a checkout.
3. **Hand Renode exactly those bytes:** `include @Tviburi.cs` before `mach create`, with the compiler cache off (`compiler-cache-enabled = False`, `RUL-10` (g)).
4. **Judge each run by its records:** a witness and an end from every guarded instance, each naming the pinned hash with `"mode":"enforcing"`, and no refusal (`RUL-12`). Renode's exit status is not a verdict.

## What it guards, and what it acknowledges

- **Guarded:** the eleven classes and their instances in the `MANIFEST`'s `class`, `restate` and `retype` lines (⟨n⟩ instances). Above `OQ-03`'s cut (ruled by Scotty 2026-11-26: the recommended cut), every path on the four flight paths is guarded or acknowledged by name: the motor, the clock tree, the sensor buses and the command links, with their DMA routes. Phase 1's gate is judged in `TVI-WO-1205.verdict.md` (⟨met⟩).
- **Acknowledged:** every ledger row whose state is `acknowledged` or `inherited-unaudited` names its reason. Read the ledger by its hash (northstar invariant 7). The field rows by state: ⟨counts from the release's `LEDGER.jsonl`⟩.
- **Not guarded:** every instance the ledger's `model` rows list without `guarded_by`. That includes TIM2–TIM17 except TIM1/TIM8, BDMA, DMAMUX2, which the stock platform does not model, the RTC, the NVIC and the watchdog.

## What the consumer must do

**The properties each guard takes** are the `MANIFEST`'s `property` lines: one per constructor parameter, `required` (the guard refuses or the platform does not load without it) or `optional` (with what absence does).
